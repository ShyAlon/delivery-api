# Check the wired money path before replacing a legacy cap

The launch guard and no-quota validator exist, but ProductionGateways constructs its paid WorkerAdmissionGateway with launch_guard=False. A documentation-level reconciliation or a validator-only fix would not protect the actual path. The own-test confirmed-cost check therefore belongs immediately before dispatch in the shared transition, independent of launch-mode selection. Preserve the old cap until the operator explicitly confirms the new runtime and atomically installs the profile while removing only that cap. Keep recent mixed-scale history blocked rather than inventing dollars. Tests must enter through the production factory as well as direct ledger transitions, and cover waiting dispatch versus settlement under the same lock order.
