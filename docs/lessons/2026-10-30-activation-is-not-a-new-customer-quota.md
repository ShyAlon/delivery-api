# Do not turn runaway protection into customer budgets

The owner approved the twelve activation proposals and emphasized allowing actions, with protection against LLM loops. Record the actual decisions, not a new guessed ceiling from two calls. Customer credits, provider expenses, own-test limits and platform tripwires are distinct.

A modern launch validator refusing owner quotas conflicts with legacy onboarding writing a $2 own-test cap. Do not fix that by silently dropping the cap or broadly relaxing validation. Keep explicit test/launch profiles and prepare an owner-reviewed transition; this documentation PR changes no runtime policy.

Approval to prepare child-mode acceptance is not paid-child activation. A preview restart is not a payment launch. Repeated manual gates are not useful loop protection, but uncertain provider outcomes still require reconciliation instead of blind replay.
