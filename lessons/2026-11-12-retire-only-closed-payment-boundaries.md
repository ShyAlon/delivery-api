# Retire a closed boundary, not its shared ledger

A default answer-level accounting path does not retire provider accounting for
Start, generator phases or local models. Trace installed callers, transport and
recovery first. Delete disconnected descriptor handoff only after proving the
replacement owns the real installed flow. Set-based Stop proof can remove an
N+1 receipt read without changing its strict boolean, owner/session and held-
usage conditions. Keep a real multi-job fail-first query-budget test beside it.
